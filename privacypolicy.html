Privacy Policy

Namaste, and welcome to Harinaam. We deeply respect your privacy and the sacredness of your spiritual journey. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding any information you may provide while using our application.

1. No Account Required

We respect your privacy. The Harinaam app does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. We have deliberately removed all login modules to ensure you can practice your devotion completely anonymously.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any external servers or databases.

All your personal app data, including your chanting progress, streaks, and preferences, is stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage or cache. This means you have full ownership and control over your data. Because the data never leaves your device, we have absolutely no access to it.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

To comply with data safety standards, please note our strict data retention and deletion practices:

  • Data Retention: Because we do not collect or transmit your data to our servers, we do not retain any of your data on our end. Your data is retained only locally on your physical device for as long as you keep the Harinaam app installed.
  • Data Deletion: Because your data is only stored locally on your device, you have complete control over its deletion. You can instantly delete all your personal data and progress at any time by simply uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's data and cache in your phone's Settings menu. Once uninstalled or cleared, the data is permanently removed.

4. Third-Party Services (Crash Reporting)

While we do not collect personal data, the app may use trusted third-party services (such as Firebase Crashlytics) solely for the purpose of ensuring the app runs smoothly. These services may collect completely anonymous, non-personal diagnostic data (like crash logs or device models) if the app crashes, helping us fix issues faster. They do not use this data to identify you.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the policy in the app and on our website.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy or how your data is handled, please don't hesitate to contact us at codewatipvtltd@gmail.com. We are here to listen and help.

May you find peace and focus in your daily practice.
