Privacy Policy

Namaste, and welcome to HariNaam – Japa & Bhakti App. We respect your privacy and want you to understand what information is stored on your device and what may be processed by services that power app features.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. No Account Required

HariNaam does not require you to create an account, register, or sign in. We do not operate a user-account database for the app. Some optional features, however, send limited information directly to third-party providers, as explained below.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores certain information locally to provide its features. This may include japa and activity progress, streaks, points, preferences, search history, AI chat history, and information used to display a local donation receipt. This information is stored in the app's local storage and is not uploaded to a HariNaam-operated account server.

You can remove local app data by clearing HariNaam's storage in your Android settings or uninstalling the app. Android device backup behavior may also depend on your device, Android version, and backup settings.

3. Approximate Location and Weather

HariNaam uses Android's coarse location permission to request approximate location for the weather feature. When location access is granted and a location is available, approximate coordinates are sent over HTTPS to OpenWeather to retrieve current weather and forecast information. The app does not need your precise GPS location for this feature.

If you deny location permission or a location is unavailable, the app uses a default weather location (Vrindavan, India). You can revoke location permission at any time in Android settings. OpenWeather processes requests under its own terms and privacy practices.

4. AI Chat Features

If you use HariNaam's AI devotional chat features, the text you submit and the context needed to answer your question may be sent over HTTPS directly to the relevant AI service provider. Depending on the feature, the app uses OpenRouter and/or NVIDIA. OpenRouter may route a prompt to an underlying model provider to generate a response.

Your AI chat history may also be saved locally on your device so you can revisit it. AI providers may process or retain submitted prompts and responses under their own terms and privacy policies; HariNaam does not control their retention practices. Please do not enter passwords, payment credentials, or sensitive personal information into AI chats.

5. Analytics, Diagnostics, Notifications, and App Configuration

HariNaam integrates Firebase services, including Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase Remote Config. Depending on the feature and SDK behavior, these services may process:

  • App interaction events, such as opening a Gita chapter, starting or completing japa, playing bhajans, opening or downloading wallpapers, and sharing content.
  • Crash reports, diagnostic information, app version, and technical details needed to understand errors and improve stability.
  • App-instance or device identifiers and a messaging registration token needed for analytics, app configuration, or delivery of notifications.

These services help us understand app usage, diagnose problems, configure app features, and deliver notifications. Data handled by Firebase is subject to Google's/Firebase's terms and privacy practices.

6. Advertising and Mediation Services

HariNaam uses Google Mobile Ads and includes mediation adapters for Meta Audience Network and AppLovin. When ads or mediation services are used, these providers and their SDKs may process information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers where available, ad impressions and interactions, app/device information, and diagnostics. Providers may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, frequency controls, and fraud prevention, depending on their configuration and your device privacy settings.

Advertising providers handle data under their own privacy policies. You can review or change available advertising and privacy controls in your Android and Google account settings, where supported.

Third-party privacy information

  • Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information
  • Firebase privacy and data processing information
  • OpenWeather
  • OpenRouter Privacy Policy
  • NVIDIA Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy

Third-party services may update their practices or policies independently. Please review their current policies for details about their data handling and retention.

7. Support and Query Forms

If you submit a query or support request in the app, you may enter your name, mobile number, email address, category, subject, and message or description. The app opens an email application with these details prepared in a message. The information is sent to our support team only if you choose an email application and send the email. Your email provider may also process the message.

We use support messages to review and respond to your request. Messages delivered to our support mailbox may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and maintain relevant support records.

8. Donations and UPI Payments

If you choose to donate, HariNaam opens a compatible UPI or payment application. The selected payment provider and your bank handle the payment. HariNaam does not ask for or store your UPI PIN, bank password, or payment credentials. The payment application may return a transaction response that the app uses to display a receipt. The payment provider's own privacy policy applies to the payment process.

9. How Information Is Shared

HariNaam does not operate its own account server or user database for the information described in this policy. However, data is sent directly from your device to third-party providers when required for the features and SDKs described above, including weather requests, AI responses, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, and advertising. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not receive AI prompts or approximate weather coordinates on a HariNaam-operated application server. Support information is delivered to us only if you choose to send the prepared email.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local app data: Remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior.
  • AI, weather, analytics, crash, messaging, and advertising data: Retention is controlled by the relevant third-party provider and its settings and policies.
  • Support emails: May be retained in the support mailbox for as long as reasonably needed to respond to and document the request.

HariNaam does not provide an in-app account or account-deletion feature because an account is not required. To manage or request deletion of information held by a third-party provider, use that provider's available privacy controls or contact the provider directly. You may contact us if you need help identifying the relevant service.

11. Security

Network requests made by the app use HTTPS where supported by the relevant service. Local app data is stored within Android's app storage environment. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and the security practices of third-party providers are governed by their own policies.

12. Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke location permission; weather will use the default location when needed.
  • AI chat is optional. You can choose not to submit messages to AI features.
  • You can clear local app data or uninstall HariNaam to remove data stored locally by the app.
  • You can manage notification, advertising, and other available privacy settings through Android or the relevant provider's settings.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the revision date shown at the top. Please review it periodically.

14. Contact Us

For privacy questions about HariNaam, contact us at codewatipvtltd@gmail.com. For in-app support requests, the app may prepare an email to support@codewati.com.

May you find peace and focus in your daily practice.
